United Delhi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026
Last updated: 29 September 2026

United Delhi is an independent Delhi public-transit information application and website. This policy explains the data the current implementation processes, why it is processed, and how privacy inquiries can be made.

Developer and privacy contact

United Delhi is the app name. Privacy inquiries may be submitted using the developer contact email shown on the United Delhi Google Play listing. Please do not send passwords, API keys, database credentials, or other secrets.

What United Delhi collects

United Delhi is designed to work without an account. The app does not currently ask for a name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifier, or payment information.

Location

Location is optional. When you choose Nearby, foreground device location is used to find nearby DTC stops and centre the map. The mobile app sends reduced-precision coordinates to the United Delhi API for nearby-stop lookup. The service does not intentionally store a user's personal location history in the transit database.

Search and journey planning

Search terms and journey-planning inputs are sent to the United Delhi API to return transit information. They are not intentionally used to create a user profile.

Technical information

Hosting, CDN, network, and security infrastructure may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, timestamps, request metadata, device/network information, and error information.

Third-party services

United Delhi uses Delhi Open Transit Data for transit information, OpenStreetMap/Leaflet for maps, Wasmer Edge for the API, Vercel for a protected realtime-feed relay, Neon for PostgreSQL storage, and GitHub for source-code/project infrastructure. These providers have their own terms and privacy policies.

Tracking and advertising

United Delhi does not currently use personalized advertising, advertising identifiers, behavioral tracking SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Nearby coordinates are processed for the requested lookup and are not intentionally inserted into the transit database. Realtime vehicle-position data may be retained for service operation and data-quality purposes. Security and infrastructure logs may be retained by infrastructure providers as required for security, operations, contracts, or law. Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or anonymized unless retention is required by law.

Children and applicable law

The app does not require an account and minimizes personal-data collection. Where applicable law requires additional safeguards for children or other users, those requirements take priority and the service will be updated as necessary.

Security

Production API traffic uses HTTPS. Server-side credentials and transit-provider API keys are kept on backend infrastructure and are not intentionally bundled into the mobile app. Rate limiting, input validation, database controls, and security response headers are used as appropriate.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, grievance redressal, and other applicable privacy rights. Use the developer contact mechanism described above for a request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, infrastructure, data practices, or applicable law changes. The date above will be updated for material changes.
