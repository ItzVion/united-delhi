United Delhi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026
Last updated: 16 October 2026

United Delhi is an independent Delhi public-transit information application and website. This policy explains the data the current implementation processes, why it is processed, and how privacy inquiries can be made.

Developer and privacy contact

United Delhi is the app name. Privacy inquiries may be submitted using vion4712@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, API keys, database credentials, or other secrets.

What United Delhi collects

United Delhi is designed to work without an account. The app does not currently ask for a name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifier, or payment information.

Location

Location is optional. When you enable it, foreground device location is used to find nearby DTC stops and centre the map. Mobile and web nearby-stop requests send coordinates rounded to four decimal places. The web client stores its map viewport rounded to three decimal places in browser local storage to restore the view; it does not save an exact device location. The app keeps device location in memory only while needed for the requested features.

Search and journey planning

Search terms and journey-planning inputs are sent to the United Delhi API to return transit information. They are not intentionally used to create a user profile.

Technical information

Hosting, CDN, network, and security infrastructure may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, timestamps, request metadata, device/network information, and error information.

Third-party services

United Delhi uses Delhi Open Transit Data (OTD) for authorised live vehicle positions and transit datasets, OpenStreetMap/Leaflet for maps, Render for the API, Neon for PostgreSQL, Vercel for the web client, and GitHub for source-code/project infrastructure. These providers have their own terms and privacy policies.

Tracking and advertising

United Delhi does not currently use personalized advertising, advertising identifiers, behavioral tracking SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Nearby coordinates are processed for the requested lookup and are not intentionally inserted into the transit database. Realtime vehicle-position data may be retained for service operation and data-quality purposes. Security and infrastructure logs may be retained by infrastructure providers as required for security, operations, contracts, or law. Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or anonymized unless retention is required by law.

Consent, children and applicable law

United Delhi does not require an age-based 18+ gate. The mobile app and website use a first-launch consent screen that asks the user to review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before continuing. The service does not require an account and minimizes personal-data collection. Where applicable law requires additional safeguards for children or other users, those requirements take priority and the service will be updated as necessary. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 form part of the compliance framework where applicable, including requirements concerning children's data and verifiable parental consent.

Security

Production API traffic uses HTTPS. Server-side credentials and transit-provider API keys are kept on backend infrastructure and are not intentionally bundled into the mobile app. Rate limiting, input validation, database controls, and security response headers are used as appropriate.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, grievance redressal, and other applicable privacy rights. Use vion4712@gmail.com for a request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, infrastructure, data practices, or applicable law changes. The date above will be updated for material changes.
